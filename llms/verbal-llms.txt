# Verbal

> Verbal (tryverbal.com), from Salusive Health, is an AI clinical compliance and quality-assurance
> platform for healthcare organizations. It audits patient interactions — phone and video calls,
> telehealth visits, AI-agent conversations and clinical chart notes — against customizable
> regulatory and quality checklists, scoring compliance and surfacing gaps for supervisors and QA
> teams. As of 2026-08-14 Verbal publishes NO public developer API, no OpenAPI or other
> machine-readable contract, no developer portal and no SDK. Its "API" language on the marketing
> site refers to Verbal consuming partner platform APIs, not to an API Verbal exposes.

## What agents should know

- There is no Verbal API to call. No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP server, A2A agent card, llms.txt or Postman collection is published on any Verbal host.
- Every /.well-known/ path 404s on the marketing host and on the application backend. The customer app host (app.tryverbal.com) answers HTTP 200 with an SPA shell for every path — those 200s are HTML, not documents, and must not be read as discovery hits.
- Access to Verbal is via the customer application (https://app.tryverbal.com) after a sales conversation, or via the Chrome extension.
- API Evangelist profile: https://apis.io/provider/verbal
